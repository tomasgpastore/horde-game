# Playtest checklist (copy results into an issue per finding)
**Feel**: movement responsive? hit feedback (flash/sound/shake) clear? levelling satisfying? first 30s hook?
**Readability**: can I see player, enemies, projectiles, pickups at 200+ enemies? damage telegraphs? UI text legible? colour contrast?
**Difficulty**: time to first death; min 1–3 reached? upgrade choices meaningful? any dominant/useless upgrade? boss fair?
**Perf**: FPS at start / 100 / 300 enemies (note GPU/CPU, browser); load time to playable; memory growth after 10 min; hitches on level-up.
**Bugs**: pause/resume, restart, tab blur, resize, console errors, stuck states, audio after first click, audio loops.
**Mobile**: touch controls, orientation, viewport/safe-area, FPS on mid phone, no scroll/zoom bounce.
**Meta**: title/OG preview correct, credits reachable, favicon, works from a fresh incognito, works in Chrome/Firefox/Safari.
